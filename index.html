Help

Stats

  • HP: Maximum health points
  • ATK: Determines damage dealt to enemies
  • DEF: Reduces incoming damage
  • CRT: Critical hit chance (%)
  • XP/LVL: Experience points and character level

Movement Controls

Keyboard:

  • W or ↑: Move up one tile
  • S or ↓: Move down one tile
  • A or ←: Move left one tile
  • D or →: Move right one tile

Mobile:

  • Swipe up, down, left, or right to move one grid in that direction

Quick Slots

  • 1: Use item in Quick Slot 1
  • 2: Use item in Quick Slot 2

Gameplay

  • Combat is turn-based (one move = one action)
  • Interact with stairs, merchants, props, or loot by moving onto the same tile
  • Attack enemies by moving adjacent to them or onto their tile
  • Destroy crates, barrels, and vases for loot and gold
